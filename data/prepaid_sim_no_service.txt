Title: Prepaid SIM Showing No Service After Recharge

Problem:
Customer's prepaid SIM shows "No Service" immediately after a successful recharge.

Possible Causes:
- SIM provisioning delay after first-time recharge on a new SIM.
- Network outage in the customer's area.
- SIM activation pending due to KYC validation.
- Incorrect APN settings on the handset.

Resolution Steps:
1. Confirm recharge is posted in CRM under customer account.
2. Ask customer to toggle Airplane Mode OFF/ON and wait 2 minutes.
3. Check network outage map for customer's pincode.
4. Verify KYC status — if pending, escalate to KYC team.
5. Guide customer to set correct APN: Settings > Mobile Networks > APN.
6. If issue persists after 30 minutes, perform remote SIM refresh from CRM.

Escalation:
Escalate to Network Operations if remote SIM refresh fails.

Tags: prepaid, SIM, no service, recharge, activation, KYC, APN
