Title: Postpaid Bill Shock - Usage Beyond Plan Limits

Problem:
Customer received a very high postpaid bill due to usage exceeding their plan's included limits.

Common Triggers:
- International roaming without add-on pack.
- Excess data usage after FUP (charged at premium rate per MB).
- Premium SMS services or IVR-based content subscriptions.

Resolution Steps:
1. Pull detailed CDR and identify the specific charges causing the spike.
2. If roaming charges: check if customer was informed about roaming rates. If not, apply goodwill waiver (one-time, per policy).
3. If data overage: check if alerts were sent at 80% and 100% data usage. If not sent (system failure), raise reversal request.
4. Advise customer to enable spend limits or usage alerts going forward.

Goodwill Waiver Limit:
Waiver up to Rs 500 per account per year, requires supervisor approval above Rs 200.
