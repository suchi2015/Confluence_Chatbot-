Title: Incorrect Bill Amount - Customer Overcharged

Problem:
Customer disputes their bill stating the charged amount is higher than expected.

Investigation Steps:
1. Pull the itemized bill from CRM for the billing period in question.
2. Compare plan charges, add-on charges, and usage charges against customer's plan details.
3. Check for any one-time charges (device EMI, activation fees, late fees).
4. Identify if any VAS or add-ons were activated without customer consent.

Resolution:
- If VAS activated without consent: deactivate immediately and raise a credit/reversal request.
- If usage charges are correct but customer wasn't aware: explain the charges and provide bill breakdown.
- If a system error caused overcharge: raise a billing correction ticket.

Reversal SLA:
Credit adjustments reflect within 24-48 hours. Confirm with customer and send SMS confirmation.

Tags: billing, overcharge, invoice, bill dispute, VAS, credit, reversal
