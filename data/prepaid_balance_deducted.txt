Title: Prepaid Balance Deducted Without Usage

Problem:
Customer reports main balance deducted even though no calls or data was used.

Root Cause:
Common causes include background app data consumption, active VAS (Value Added Services), or roaming charges if SIM was used outside home network.

Resolution Steps:
1. Pull usage CDR from CRM for the date range in question.
2. Check active VAS subscriptions under customer profile.
3. If VAS found, deactivate and apply a one-time reversal if charge was within 24 hours.
4. Advise customer to check background data usage on device.
5. If CDR shows no usage but balance still deducted, raise a billing discrepancy ticket.

Reversal Policy:
Balance reversal is allowed once per 90 days per account, for charges up to Rs 50.
