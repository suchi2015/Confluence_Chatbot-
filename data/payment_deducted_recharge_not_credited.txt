Title: Payment Deducted But Recharge Not Credited

Problem:
Customer made a payment but recharge was not credited to the account.

Root Cause:
This can occur due to payment gateway timeout, bank processing delays, or technical glitches during transaction processing.

Solution Steps:
1. Ask customer for transaction ID or payment screenshot.
2. Check payment status in billing portal under Transactions > Pending.
3. If payment shows SUCCESS at bank but not credited, raise a manual credit request via CRM > Billing > Manual Recharge.
4. Inform customer — credit will reflect within 2-4 hours.
5. If payment shows FAILED, ask customer to retry or use an alternate payment method.

Escalation:
If not resolved within 4 hours, escalate to Level 2 Billing Team with transaction ID and customer number.

Tags: payment, recharge, prepaid, deducted, not credited, transaction failed
