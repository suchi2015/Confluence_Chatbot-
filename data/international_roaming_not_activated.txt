Title: International Roaming Not Activated

Problem:
Customer is traveling internationally but roaming service is not active on their number.

Eligibility Check:
- Account must have no outstanding bill beyond grace period.
- Account must be active (not barred or suspended).
- For prepaid: minimum balance requirement (typically Rs 200+).

Activation Steps:
1. Verify account eligibility in CRM.
2. Activate International Roaming from CRM > Services > Roaming > Enable.
3. Ask customer to toggle Airplane Mode OFF/ON after activation.
4. Recommend purchasing an IR pack for the destination country to avoid high pay-per-use rates.

Available IR Packs:
Daily packs (Rs 149/day), Weekly packs (Rs 999 for 7 days), Country-specific packs available via app or store.
