Title: Slow Internet Speed - 4G Showing But Speed is Low

Problem:
Customer's device shows 4G/LTE icon but actual download/upload speed is very slow (below 1 Mbps).

Possible Causes:
- Data plan exhausted — FUP (Fair Usage Policy) throttling active.
- Network congestion in the customer's cell tower area.
- Incorrect APN configuration.
- Device-level issue (cache, settings).

Resolution Steps:
1. Check data balance — if FUP hit, advise customer to purchase a data add-on pack.
2. Check tower congestion report for the customer's pincode.
3. Ask customer to run a speed test at fast.com and share results.
4. Guide APN reset: Settings > Reset Network Settings.
5. Ask customer to restart the device and test again.
6. If congestion is the cause, note the ticket and advise improvement expected within 24 hours.
