Title: VPN Connection Issue - Troubleshooting Guide

Problem:
Employee is unable to connect to the company VPN. The VPN client shows error or connection times out.

Root Cause:
This usually happens due to incorrect VPN credentials, outdated VPN client, or firewall blocking the connection.

Solution Steps:
1. Check your internet connection first - open a browser and visit any website.
2. Open the VPN client (Cisco AnyConnect or GlobalProtect).
3. Make sure the server address is correct: vpn.company.com
4. Re-enter your credentials - use your Windows login username and password.
5. If still failing, go to Control Panel > Programs > Uninstall the VPN client.
6. Download the latest VPN client from IT portal: http://itportal.company.com/vpn
7. Reinstall and try connecting again.
8. If issue persists, check Windows Firewall - temporarily disable and test.
9. Contact IT helpdesk if none of the above steps work: helpdesk@company.com
