Title: Password Reset and Account Unlock Guide

Problem:
Employee forgot their Windows/email password or account is locked due to multiple failed login attempts.

Root Cause:
Account locks automatically after 5 failed login attempts as per company security policy.

Solution Steps:
1. Go to the self-service password reset portal: https://passwordreset.company.com
2. Enter your employee ID or registered email address.
3. You will receive an OTP on your registered mobile number.
4. Enter the OTP and set a new password.
5. Password must be: minimum 8 characters, 1 uppercase, 1 number, 1 special character.
6. If account is locked (not just password forgotten):
   - Call IT helpdesk: 1800-XXX-XXXX (available 24/7)
   - Or raise a ticket at: http://itportal.company.com/tickets
   - IT will unlock within 30 minutes during business hours.
7. After reset, update password in all devices (phone, laptop, email client).
8. If you use Outlook, go to File > Account Settings > update password there too.
