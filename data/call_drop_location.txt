Title: Calls Dropping in Specific Location

Problem:
Customer experiences repeated call drops in a specific building, locality, or area.

Root Cause Analysis:
- Poor indoor signal due to building material (thick concrete, metal).
- Cell tower under maintenance or overloaded.
- Dead zone — outside network coverage.

Resolution Steps:
1. Verify coverage for customer's pincode using the network coverage tool in CRM.
2. Check for any active tower outages or maintenance in the area.
3. Ask customer to try in a different spot (near window) to rule out building interference.
4. Suggest enabling Wi-Fi Calling if the device supports it.
5. If the area is a legitimate dead zone, log a coverage improvement request.

Wi-Fi Calling Setup:
Android: Settings > Connections > Wi-Fi Calling > Enable.
iPhone: Settings > Phone > Wi-Fi Calling > Enable.

Tags: call drop, network, coverage, signal, Wi-Fi calling, dead zone
