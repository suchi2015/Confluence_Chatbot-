Title: UPI Payment Failing on MyOperator App

Problem:
Customer's UPI payment is failing when trying to recharge or pay bill through the app.

Possible Causes:
- UPI daily transaction limit reached.
- Bank server downtime.
- Incorrect UPI PIN entered 3 times (account temporarily locked by bank).
- App-level payment gateway issue.

Resolution Steps:
1. Ask customer to try a different UPI app (GPay, PhonePe) to isolate if the issue is app-specific.
2. Check if the customer's bank is listed in current payment gateway downtime alerts.
3. Advise customer to check UPI transaction limit with their bank.
4. If issue is app-specific, collect details and raise a bug report to the app team.
5. Suggest alternate payment methods: debit/credit card, net banking, or offline recharge via store.

Tags: UPI, payment, app, transaction, GPay, PhonePe, gateway, recharge
