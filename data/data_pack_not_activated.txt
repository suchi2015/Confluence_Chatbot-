Title: Data Pack Not Activated After Purchase

Problem:
Customer purchased a data add-on pack but it is not showing as active and data is being deducted from main balance.

Resolution Steps:
1. Verify purchase in CRM > Add-Ons > Active Packs.
2. If pack shows in CRM but not on device: ask customer to dial *123# to check balance and active packs.
3. Restart device — sometimes pack activation requires network refresh.
4. If pack not in CRM despite payment: check payment status and process manual activation.
5. Ensure the data pack is compatible with customer's base plan.

Common Conflicts:
Some promotional data packs are not compatible with legacy plans. Verify pack eligibility before activation.

Tags: data pack, add-on, activation, prepaid, postpaid, balance, *123#
