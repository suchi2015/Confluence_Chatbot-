Title: Customer Unable to Login to Self-Care App

Problem:
Customer cannot log into the MyOperator self-care app or web portal.

Common Causes:
- Forgotten password.
- Account locked after multiple failed attempts.
- OTP not received for SMS-based login.
- App version outdated.

Resolution Steps:
1. Guide customer to use Forgot Password and reset via registered email or OTP.
2. If account locked: unlock from CRM > Customer Profile > Account Status.
3. If OTP not received: check if mobile number is correct and network is available. Retry OTP after 60 seconds.
4. Ask customer to update app from Play Store or App Store if version is older than 3 months.
5. Clear app cache if app crashes after login: Settings > Apps > MyOperator > Clear Cache.

Tags: account, login, password, OTP, app, self-care portal, locked
